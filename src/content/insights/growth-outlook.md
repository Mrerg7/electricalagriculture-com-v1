summary: Market research projects sustained double-digit growth for electric tractors as battery costs fall and policy support expands.

Forecasts for the electric tractor segment into the 2026–2035 window consistently cite strong CAGR — often above **20%** in leading regional analyses — driven by battery improvements, emissions policy, and farm operating-cost pressure on diesel.
