---
title: Well-to-Wheels Efficiency Leap
summary: Electrified equipment can nearly double energy efficiency versus combustion baselines in suitable duty cycles.
source: Industry analyses referenced in public briefings, early 2026
order: 1
---

Publicly reported ranges place electrified farm machinery at roughly **39–72%** well-to-wheels efficiency versus **25–37%** for conventional engines — a structural advantage when electricity is generated cleanly and machines are used within battery-friendly envelopes.
