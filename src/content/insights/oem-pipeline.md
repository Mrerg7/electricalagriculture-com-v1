New Holland’s all-electric Farmall-class offerings, Fendt’s e100 lineage, Kubota and Case IH activity, plus specialists like Monarch and Solectrac, show a clear commercialization path: prove reliability on orchards, dairies, municipalities, and specialty farms before scaling horsepower.
