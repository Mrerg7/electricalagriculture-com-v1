---
title: Practical Challenges
summary: Battery mass, soil compaction, range, and price still slow full-scale electrification of heavy field work.
label: Reality
order: 4
---

Electrification is not frictionless. Compact and utility tractors gain traction from battery mass; large combines and high-horsepower row-crop machines face weight, soil-health, and energy-density constraints that keep diesel dominant for long days in distant fields.

Reliability expectations remain high: farmers need machines that finish the season. Charging logistics, capital cost, and residual-value uncertainty continue to shape buyer decisions even where total cost of ownership looks favorable on paper.

The near-term opportunity is selective electrification — the right machine, the right duty cycle, the right farm energy plan — not a blanket replacement of every diesel fleet overnight.
