---
title: Electrified Farm Machinery
summary: Diesel-to-electric powertrains are reshaping heavy equipment efficiency, maintenance, and autonomy readiness.
label: Machinery
order: 1
---

The agricultural sector is rapidly transitioning from diesel to electric powertrains to meet decarbonization goals, with significant developments in heavy machinery efficiency.

As of early 2026, electrified tractors and farm equipment are demonstrating **39% to 72% well-to-wheels energy efficiency**, a substantial improvement over the **25% to 37%** efficiency typical of traditional combustion engines.

Despite higher upfront costs, these machines offer lower maintenance requirements due to fewer moving parts and are increasingly compatible with autonomous systems. Advanced high-voltage components, connectors, and battery packs are enabling quiet, instant-torque operation suited to orchard work, utility tasks, and emerging full-day field cycles.
