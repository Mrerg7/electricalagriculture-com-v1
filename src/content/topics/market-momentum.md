title: Market Momentum
summary: Electric tractor adoption is climbing as OEMs and startups race to commercialize battery and hybrid platforms.
label: Market
order: 2
---

Electric farming equipment sits within a broader industrial electrification wave spanning agriculture, construction, and mining — a multi-billion-dollar equipment category through the next decade.

Battery-electric platforms currently lead agricultural electric tractor revenue share, with lithium-ion chemistries dominating. North America and global forecasts point to double-digit compound growth into the mid-2030s as duty-cycle fit improves and charging infrastructure expands on farms.

OEMs and specialists — including New Holland, Fendt, Kubota, Monarch Tractor, Solectrac, and John Deere’s electrification roadmap — are bringing utility-class and compact electric models to market first, while higher-horsepower segments remain a longer engineering horizon.
